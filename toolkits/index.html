Email Template

Subject: Request for district AI transparency and public disclosure Hello [District Contact], I’m writing with a straightforward transparency request about AI use in the district. Could you please share: 1) Which AI-enabled tools the district currently uses in instructional or administrative contexts 2) What student data those tools process 3) Whether AI can influence high-impact decisions such as grading, discipline, counseling, placement, attendance intervention, or monitoring 4) Whether human review exists before serious consequences are imposed 5) Where the district publishes AI policies, guidance, or vendor information If this information is already public, a link is perfect. Thank you, [Name] [Role / School / City]

Board Template

Hello Board Members, I’m asking the district to publish a basic AI transparency page so students and families can understand what AI tools are used, what student data is processed, and what human review exists. We are not anti-AI. We are pro-transparency. Public schools should be able to answer basic questions about AI use, student data, and human review. I’m asking the board to: 1) Review current district AI disclosure practices 2) Consider adopting a public AI tool registry and notice process 3) Ensure serious AI-influenced decisions include meaningful human review Thank you.

Records Template

Subject: Public records request regarding district AI policies and vendor contracts Hello [Records Officer / District Contact], Under applicable public records law, I am requesting copies of the following records: 1) Policies, guidance, memoranda, or administrative procedures referencing artificial intelligence, automated decision tools, or generative AI 2) Contracts, procurement documents, or amendments for AI-enabled software used by the district 3) Lists or inventories of AI-enabled tools used in instructional, disciplinary, counseling, administrative, assessment, monitoring, or student support contexts 4) Documents describing student data collected, processed, retained, or shared by those tools If any of these records are already public, please provide the links instead. Thank you, [Name]
