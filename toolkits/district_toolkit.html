District Implementation Toolkit

“Minimum viable transparency” → publish in 48 hours

This is a plug-and-play checklist. Publishing these items creates the public AI governance signals your index measures.

48-hour transparency bundle

  • AI Disclosure Page: tools + purpose + data categories + vendor links.
  • AI Oversight Owner: named role + contact alias (example: ai-governance@district.org).
  • Approval pathway: short note on how AI tools are reviewed/approved (who signs off).

30-day governance baseline

  • Staff guidance: allowed uses, prohibited uses, privacy expectations, and escalation steps.
  • Student guidance: how AI can be used for learning without academic integrity issues.
  • Annual review cadence: “We review AI inventory + disclosure each semester / year.”

60-day board-level signal

  • Board policy mention or addendum referencing AI (even brief is fine).
  • Publish meeting minutes / agenda item showing AI governance discussion.
  • Post a “Transparency & AI” page under the district “Policies” or “Technology” section.

Recommended page title (for discoverability)

  • “Artificial Intelligence (AI) Use and Transparency”

That exact phrase improves searchability for parents and journalists.
