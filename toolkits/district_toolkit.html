District Implementation Toolkit

A minimum viable transparency baseline districts can publish quickly without overhauling their entire AI program.

This is a plug-and-play checklist for the disclosure signals the index measures.

48-hour transparency bundle

  • AI Disclosure Page: tools, purpose, data categories, and vendor links.
  • AI Oversight Owner: named role and contact alias, such as ai-governance@district.org.
  • Approval pathway: a short note on how AI tools are reviewed and who signs off.

30-day governance baseline

  • Staff guidance covering allowed uses, prohibited uses, privacy expectations, and escalation steps.
  • Student guidance explaining how AI can be used for learning without academic integrity issues.
  • Annual review cadence for AI inventory and disclosure updates.

60-day board-level signal

  • Board policy mention or addendum referencing AI, even if brief.
  • Published meeting minutes or agenda items showing AI governance discussion.
  • A district page titled “Transparency & AI” or similar under policies or technology.

Recommended page title

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Use and Transparency

That exact phrase improves searchability for parents, reporters, and school board members.
