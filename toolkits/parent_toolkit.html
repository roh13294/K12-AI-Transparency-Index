Parent Toolkit

Copy-paste steps to request clear AI disclosure and governance from your district.

Use this if your district scores low or has missing public signals.

What to ask for (short list)

  • Which AI tools are used (instructional + administrative)
  • Where AI is used (classroom, grading, counseling, discipline, communications)
  • What student data is involved
  • Who is accountable (named AI governance point of contact)
  • Where policies live (board policy / admin regs mentioning AI)

Email template (copy-paste)

Subject: Request for public AI disclosure and district AI governance point of contact Hello [Superintendent/Team], I’m a parent/guardian in the district. I’m writing to request a public-facing disclosure of the district’s AI use and governance. Could you please share: 1) A list of AI / automated decision tools currently used by the district (instructional and administrative) 2) Any board policy / administrative regulation that governs AI use (or confirm if none exists) 3) The district’s named point of contact responsible for AI governance and parent questions 4) Where parents can find ongoing updates on AI tools, vendors, and data handling If a disclosure page already exists, please reply with the link. Thank you, [Name] [School / City]

If they don’t reply

  • Follow up once after 7–10 days.
  • Send to the Board of Education email (often listed on the district site).
  • If your state has FOIA/records requests, you can request vendor contracts and AI-related policies.
This toolkit is informational and designed to reduce friction for families seeking clarity.
