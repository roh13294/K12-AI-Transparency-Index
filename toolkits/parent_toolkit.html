What to ask for

  • Which AI tools are used in instructional and administrative settings
  • Where AI is used across classrooms, grading, counseling, discipline, and communications
  • What student data is involved
  • Who is accountable as the named AI governance point of contact
  • Where the district publishes policies or board language mentioning AI

Email template

Subject: Request for public AI disclosure and district AI governance point of contact Hello [Superintendent/Team], I’m a parent/guardian in the district. I’m writing to request a public-facing disclosure of the district’s AI use and governance. Could you please share: 1) A list of AI / automated decision tools currently used by the district (instructional and administrative) 2) Any board policy / administrative regulation that governs AI use (or confirm if none exists) 3) The district’s named point of contact responsible for AI governance and parent questions 4) Where parents can find ongoing updates on AI tools, vendors, and data handling If a disclosure page already exists, please reply with the link. Thank you, [Name] [School / City]

If they don’t reply

  • Follow up once after 7 to 10 days.
  • Send the same request to the Board of Education email if it is listed publicly.
  • If your state has public records laws, request vendor contracts and AI-related policies.
