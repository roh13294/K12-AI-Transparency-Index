Methodology
2026 National K–12 AI Transparency Index (K12-AITI)
Purpose

The 2026 National K–12 AI Transparency Index evaluates publicly accessible governance signals related to artificial intelligence use within U.S. public school districts. The Index measures transparency, not internal implementation practices.

This review is limited to information available on official district websites at the time of data collection.

Scope

353 public school districts

6 U.S. states: Michigan, California, Texas, Florida, New York, Illinois

Data collection period: 2026

Source: Public-facing district websites only

Private internal policies, vendor agreements, and unpublished documents were not evaluated.

Data Collection Method

District websites were programmatically scanned using an automated crawler that:

Began at the official district homepage

Identified publicly linked pages containing potential governance or policy content

Extracted text from HTML and accessible PDF documents

Flagged evidence of predefined transparency criteria

Each district was subject to a fixed crawl depth and time limit to ensure consistent treatment.

Scoring Framework

Each district is evaluated using a 100-point weighted index based on five binary criteria.

Criterion	Weight
Public AI Policy Exists	30
AI Use Publicly Disclosed	25
Named Oversight Authority	20
Board-Level AI Reference	15
Public AI Governance Contact Pathway	10

Total possible score: 100

Each criterion is scored 0 or 1 based on identifiable public evidence.

Tier Classification
Score Range	Tier
80–100	Leading Transparency
60–79	Emerging Governance
40–59	Limited Disclosure
20–39	Minimal Transparency
0–19	No Public AI Governance Signals
Interpretation Notes

The Index measures public transparency signals only.

A low score does not imply a district lacks internal governance practices.

A high score does not evaluate policy quality or effectiveness.

Scores reflect publicly accessible documentation at time of review.

Re-evaluation Process

Districts may request re-evaluation if:

Additional publicly accessible documentation exists

Policies were published after data collection

Links were missed due to crawl limitations

All revisions will be documented transparently in future updates.

Limitations

Automated text analysis may not capture all nuanced language.

Crawl time limits may restrict discovery of deeply nested documents.

District websites vary significantly in structure and accessibility.

The Index is intended as a baseline transparency measurement and will evolve in future annual releases.
