2026 National Review Finds 96% of School Districts Show Limited or No Public AI Governance Transparency

A new cross-state review of 353 public school districts across Michigan, California, Texas, Florida, New York, and Illinois finds that the vast majority provide little or no publicly accessible documentation regarding artificial intelligence governance practices.

The 2026 National K–12 AI Transparency Index evaluates whether districts publish AI-related policies, disclose AI tool usage, identify oversight authority, reference AI governance at the board level, and provide public contact pathways related to AI oversight.

Key findings:

96% of districts demonstrate minimal transparency or no publicly accessible AI governance signals.

23% show no identifiable public AI governance signals.

73% demonstrate limited transparency.

4% show emerging governance practices.

No district in the sample met the threshold for leading transparency.

The Index measures public transparency only and does not assess internal implementation practices.

As AI tools become increasingly integrated into educational workflows, curriculum development, administrative systems, and student-facing platforms, governance transparency may play a critical role in maintaining public trust.

The 2026 Index establishes a baseline measurement. Districts may request re-evaluation if additional public documentation exists.

Full methodology and state-level findings are available upon request.
