Methodology

This index measures publicly visible AI transparency and governance signals on district websites. It does not claim to detect internal practices that are not publicly disclosed.

Back to search

What we look for

  • Public AI policy or board policy language that references generative AI or automated decision tools
  • Public disclosure of AI use guidelines for staff and students
  • Named oversight, ownership, or accountability role for AI use
  • Board policy mentions of AI or governance signals
  • Public contact pathway for questions or concerns

Score interpretation

  • 0: No public AI governance signals found
  • 10: Minimal transparency signals
  • 30: Limited disclosure
  • 60+: Emerging to leading transparency

Important limitations

  • This is website based. If a district has internal policy but does not publish it, it may score low.
  • Results depend on what districts choose to post publicly and how their sites are structured.
  • Sampling is limited per state and is designed to create a national baseline.

Why this matters

Schools increasingly use AI powered tools across education and operations. Families deserve clear, accessible disclosure. Transparency is the first step toward accountability.
