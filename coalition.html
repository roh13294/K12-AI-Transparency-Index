Students for K–12 AI Transparency

A national student-led coalition making AI use in schools visible, understandable, and accountable.

Built on the K–12 AI Transparency Index, which tracks public AI transparency signals across 1,913 districts in all 50 states, our coalition helps students, parents, educators, and policymakers advocate for AI disclosure, student data protections, and human review.

We are not anti-AI. We are pro-transparency.

Why This Coalition Exists

Schools are adopting AI tools faster than most students and families can understand them. In many districts, it is difficult to find basic public information about what AI tools are used, what student data is processed, whether AI can influence high-impact decisions, and whether human review exists. This coalition exists to make those questions public, organized, and impossible to ignore.

What We Are Fighting For

Clear, practical transparency protections

Public AI tool registries

Districts should publish a current public list of AI-enabled tools used across school operations.

Student and family notice

Students and families deserve notice when AI is being used in high-impact educational contexts.

Human review before serious consequences

No student should face a major educational consequence from an AI-influenced decision without a real person reviewing it.

Appeal rights for AI-influenced decisions

Families should have a clear path to request explanation, correction, and reconsideration.

Student data protections

Districts should explain what student data AI tools process and what guardrails apply.

Annual district AI transparency reports

Communities need a public yearly snapshot of AI tools, policies, complaints, and changes.

Vendor contract disclosure requirements

Public schools should be able to answer basic questions about vendor obligations and data protections.
