Student AI Transparency and Human Review Act

A model state framework for AI disclosure, student data protections, and human review in schools.

We are developing a model state bill requiring public school districts to disclose AI systems used in schools, protect student data, and guarantee human review before serious AI-influenced consequences.

No student should face a major educational consequence from an AI-influenced decision without notice, explanation, and meaningful human review.

Key Provisions

Core transparency and due process protections

Public AI tools registry

Districts should publish a baseline list of AI systems used in schools.

Disclosure of high-impact AI uses

Communities deserve clarity when AI is used in grading, discipline, placement, or similar contexts.

Notice to students and families

Families should receive timely notice before AI shapes serious educational decisions.

Human review before serious consequences

Meaningful human review must exist before AI influences a major outcome.

Appeal process

Students and families should be able to request explanation, correction, and reconsideration.

Student data protections

Student information should not be repurposed or exploited without clear authority and safeguards.

Vendor contract transparency

Districts should disclose basic vendor obligations tied to AI systems used with students.

Annual district AI transparency reports

Every district should publish a yearly summary of tools, uses, complaints, and policy updates.

12-month implementation timeline

The bill is designed to be practical, with a clear rollout window for districts.

Why This Is Narrow and Practical

This proposal does not ban AI in schools. It focuses on basic public disclosure, human review, appeal rights, and student data safeguards. Districts would have time to comply, and the policy is designed to help schools use AI responsibly while giving students and families clear information.

Who This Helps

Transparency helps each part of the school ecosystem differently

Students

Students gain clearer notice, stronger protections, and a path to human review when AI affects serious outcomes.

Parents and guardians

Families can better understand which tools are used, what data is processed, and where to raise questions.

Teachers

Teachers benefit from clearer district expectations and more confidence about approved uses and safeguards.

School boards

Boards gain a practical governance structure they can oversee without banning classroom innovation.

State lawmakers

Lawmakers get a narrow, implementation-oriented framework that centers disclosure and due process.

District administrators

Administrators get a clear baseline for disclosure, review processes, and public communication.

For Lawmakers

The proposal is designed to be narrow, practical, and pro-transparency. It does not ban AI in schools. It requires districts to provide basic public information, protect student data, and ensure students and families have human review when AI affects serious decisions.
